# ADR NNNN — Decision Title

- **Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded | Deprecated
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

Describe the situation, the forces at play, and the problem to be decided. What needs, what constraints, what tradeoffs? Stay factual: where are we starting from, and why must we decide now?

## Decision

Clearly state the chosen option, in present tense and active voice ("We do X"). One decision per ADR.

## Consequences

List what follows from the decision: benefits, costs, new constraints, what becomes possible or impossible. Include neutral or negative consequences, not just positive ones.

## Alternatives Considered

For each serious alternative: what it consisted of, and why it was rejected.
